
# EXERCISE 4 – DOT PRODUCT AND PERPENDICULARITY

## Given

The vectors are:

$$
u=(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
v=(2,-1,3)
$$

Compute the dot product u · v and determine whether the vectors are perpendicular.

Then find a value of a so that:

$$
w=(2,-1,a)
$$

is perpendicular to u, and check the result.

## Solution

### Step 1 – Calculate the Dot Product

The dot product of two vectors is calculated by multiplying their corresponding components and adding the results.

The formula is:

$$
u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2+u_3v_3
$$

Given:

$$
u=(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
v=(2,-1,3)
$$

Substitute the components:

$$
u\cdot v=1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(3)
$$

Calculate:

$$
u\cdot v=2-2-3
$$

Therefore:

$$
u\cdot v=-3
$$

### Step 2 – Determine Whether the Vectors Are Perpendicular

Two vectors are perpendicular if and only if their dot product equals zero.

The condition is:

$$
u\cdot v=0
$$

However, we calculated:

$$
u\cdot v=-3
$$

Since:

$$
-3\neq 0
$$

The vectors are not perpendicular.

### Step 3 – Find the Value of a

We are given:

$$
w=(2,-1,a)
$$

We want w to be perpendicular to u.

Therefore, their dot product must equal zero:

$$
u\cdot w=0
$$

Using:

$$
u=(1,2,-1)
$$

Calculate the dot product:

$$
1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(a)=0
$$

Simplify:

$$
2-2-a=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
-a=0
$$

Solving for a:

$$
a=0
$$

### Step 4 – Check the Result

Substitute a=0 into the vector w:

$$
w=(2,-1,0)
$$

Now calculate the dot product with u:

$$
u\cdot w=1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(0)
$$

Simplify:

$$
u\cdot w=2-2+0
$$

Therefore:

$$
u\cdot w=0
$$

The dot product is zero, so the vectors are perpendicular.

### Final Answers

The dot product of the original vectors is:

$$
u\cdot v=-3
$$

Therefore, u and v are not perpendicular.

The required value of a is:

$$
a=0
$$

The resulting vector is:

$$
w=(2,-1,0)
$$

Verification:

$$
u\cdot w=0
$$

Therefore, u and w are perpendicular.

### Conclusion

The dot product provides a simple way to determine whether two vectors are perpendicular.

If the dot product is zero, the vectors are perpendicular.

If the dot product is nonzero, they are not perpendicular.

In this exercise, the original vectors are not perpendicular, but choosing a=0 makes the new vector w perpendicular to u.
