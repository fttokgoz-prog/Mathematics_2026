
# EXERCISE 5 – ANGLE BETWEEN VECTORS

## Given

The vectors are:

$$
u=(1,1,0)
$$

$$
v=(1,0,1)
$$

We need to:

1. Use the sign of u · v to predict the type of angle.
2. Compute the angle.
3. Compare the result with the prediction.

## Solution

### Step 1 – Calculate the Dot Product

The dot product formula is:

$$
u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2+u_3v_3
$$

Substitute the given vectors:

$$
u\cdot v=1(1)+1(0)+0(1)
$$

Simplify:

$$
u\cdot v=1+0+0
$$

Therefore:

$$
u\cdot v=1
$$

### Step 2 – Predict the Type of Angle

The sign of the dot product tells us the type of angle between two nonzero vectors.

- If the dot product is positive, the angle is acute.
- If the dot product is zero, the angle is 90 degrees.
- If the dot product is negative, the angle is obtuse.

In our case:

$$
u\cdot v=1>0
$$

Therefore, we predict that the angle is acute.

### Step 3 – Calculate the Lengths of Both Vectors

The angle formula requires the lengths of both vectors.

For vector u:

$$
|u|=\sqrt{1^2+1^2+0^2}
$$

$$
|u|=\sqrt{2}
$$

For vector v:

$$
|v|=\sqrt{1^2+0^2+1^2}
$$

$$
|v|=\sqrt{2}
$$

Therefore:

$$
|u|=\sqrt{2},\quad |v|=\sqrt{2}
$$

### Step 4 – Calculate the Angle

The formula for the angle between two vectors is:

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{u\cdot v}{|u||v|}
$$

Substitute the calculated values:

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{2}}
$$

Simplify:

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{1}{2}
$$

To find the angle, take the inverse cosine:

$$
\theta=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)
$$

Therefore:

$$
\theta=60^\circ
$$

### Step 5 – Compare the Result with the Prediction

In Step 2, we predicted that the angle would be acute because the dot product was positive.

The calculated angle is:

$$
\theta=60^\circ
$$

Since:

$$
0^\circ<60^\circ<90^\circ
$$

The angle is acute.

Therefore, the calculation confirms our original prediction.

### Final Answer

The dot product is:

$$
u\cdot v=1
$$

The angle between the vectors is:

$$
\theta=60^\circ
$$

The angle is acute.

### Conclusion

The sign of the dot product provides information about the angle between two nonzero vectors before calculating its exact value.

A positive dot product indicates an acute angle, a zero dot product indicates a right angle, and a negative dot product indicates an obtuse angle.

In this exercise, the positive dot product correctly predicts an acute angle of 60 degrees.
