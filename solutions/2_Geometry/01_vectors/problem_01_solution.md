
# EXERCISE 1 – VECTOR OPERATIONS AND THEIR MEANING

## Given

For the vectors:

$$
u=(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
v=(-1,4,2)
$$

Compute:

$$
u+v,\quad u-v,\quad 3u-2v
$$

Then verify that (u+v)-v=u, and explain geometrically what adding two vectors means.

## Solution

### Step 1 – Calculate the Sum of the Vectors

To add two vectors, add their corresponding components.

Given:

$$
u=(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
v=(-1,4,2)
$$

Therefore:

$$
u+v=(2+(-1),-1+4,3+2)
$$

Simplify:

$$
u+v=(1,3,5)
$$

### Step 2 – Calculate the Difference of the Vectors

To subtract two vectors, subtract their corresponding components.

$$
u-v=(2-(-1),-1-4,3-2)
$$

Simplify:

$$
u-v=(3,-5,1)
$$

### Step 3 – Calculate 3u - 2v

First, multiply each vector by its scalar.

For vector u:

$$
3u=3(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
3u=(6,-3,9)
$$

For vector v:

$$
2v=2(-1,4,2)
$$

$$
2v=(-2,8,4)
$$

Now subtract the vectors:

$$
3u-2v=(6,-3,9)-(-2,8,4)
$$

Subtract the corresponding components:

$$
3u-2v=(6+2,-3-8,9-4)
$$

Therefore:

$$
3u-2v=(8,-11,5)
$$

### Step 4 – Verify That (u+v)-v=u

We already calculated:

$$
u+v=(1,3,5)
$$

Now subtract v:

$$
(u+v)-v=(1,3,5)-(-1,4,2)
$$

Subtract the corresponding components:

$$
(u+v)-v=(1+1,3-4,5-2)
$$

Therefore:

$$
(u+v)-v=(2,-1,3)
$$

Since:

$$
u=(2,-1,3)
$$

We can confirm that:

$$
(u+v)-v=u
$$

The identity is verified.

### Step 5 – Geometrical Explanation of Vector Addition

Adding two vectors represents combining two displacements.

Geometrically, vector addition can be understood using the head-to-tail method.

1. Draw the first vector u.
2. Place the starting point of vector v at the endpoint of vector u.
3. Draw a new vector from the starting point of u to the endpoint of v.

This new vector represents the sum u+v.

The same result can also be obtained using the parallelogram method.

The direction and length of the resulting vector depend on the directions and magnitudes of the original vectors.

Vector addition is also useful for representing combined movements or forces.

### Final Answers

The calculated vectors are:

**Vector addition:**

$$
u+v=(1,3,5)
$$

**Vector subtraction:**

$$
u-v=(3,-5,1)
$$

**Scalar multiplication and subtraction:**

$$
3u-2v=(8,-11,5)
$$

**Identity verification:**

$$
(u+v)-v=u=(2,-1,3)
$$

### Conclusion

Vector addition and subtraction are performed component by component.

Scalar multiplication changes the magnitude of a vector and can also reverse its direction when the scalar is negative.

Geometrically, adding two vectors combines their displacements into one resulting vector.
