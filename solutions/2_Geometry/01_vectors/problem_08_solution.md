
# EXERCISE 8 – LINEAR COMBINATION AND CHECKING THE RESULT

## Given

The vectors are:

$$
w=(5,1)
$$

$$
u=(1,1)
$$

$$
v=(2,-1)
$$

Check whether w can be written as:

$$
w=au+bv
$$

If possible, find a and b, reconstruct w, and explain the geometric meaning of these coefficients.

## Solution

### Step 1 – Write the Linear Combination

We are given:

$$
w=au+bv
$$

Substitute the vectors:

$$
(5,1)=a(1,1)+b(2,-1)
$$

Multiply each vector by its coefficient:

$$
(5,1)=(a,a)+(2b,-b)
$$

Add the corresponding components:

$$
(5,1)=(a+2b,a-b)
$$

### Step 2 – Create a System of Equations

By comparing the corresponding components, we obtain:

$$
a+2b=5
$$

$$
a-b=1
$$

Now we solve this system to find a and b.

### Step 3 – Solve the System

From the second equation:

$$
a-b=1
$$

Rearrange:

$$
a=1+b
$$

Substitute this expression into the first equation:

$$
(1+b)+2b=5
$$

Simplify:

$$
1+3b=5
$$

Subtract 1 from both sides:

$$
3b=4
$$

Therefore:

$$
b=\frac{4}{3}
$$

Now substitute b into the second equation:

$$
a-\frac{4}{3}=1
$$

Add 4/3 to both sides:

$$
a=1+\frac{4}{3}
$$

Therefore:

$$
a=\frac{7}{3}
$$

### Step 4 – Reconstruct the Vector w

We found:

$$
a=\frac{7}{3}
$$

$$
b=\frac{4}{3}
$$

Substitute these values into the original expression:

$$
w=\frac{7}{3}u+\frac{4}{3}v
$$

Using the given vectors:

$$
w=\frac{7}{3}(1,1)+\frac{4}{3}(2,-1)
$$

Multiply the vectors:

$$
w=\left(\frac{7}{3},\frac{7}{3}\right)
+\left(\frac{8}{3},-\frac{4}{3}\right)
$$

Add the corresponding components:

$$
w=\left(\frac{15}{3},\frac{3}{3}\right)
$$

Simplify:

$$
w=(5,1)
$$

This is exactly the original vector.

### Step 5 – Verify the Result

The reconstructed vector is:

$$
w=(5,1)
$$

The given vector is also:

$$
w=(5,1)
$$

Therefore, the result is correct.

The coefficients satisfy both equations:

$$
\frac{7}{3}+2\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)=5
$$

$$
\frac{7}{3}-\frac{4}{3}=1
$$

Both equations are satisfied.

### Step 6 – Explain the Geometric Meaning

A linear combination represents a vector constructed by multiplying other vectors by scalar coefficients and adding the results.

In this exercise:

$$
w=\frac{7}{3}u+\frac{4}{3}v
$$

This means that the vector w can be obtained by combining the directions of u and v.

Geometrically, the coefficients determine how much of each vector contributes to the resulting vector.

Since u and v are not parallel, they provide two different directions in the plane.

Therefore, they can be combined to represent the vector w.

The coefficients are unique because the two given vectors are linearly independent.

### Final Answer

The required coefficients are:

$$
a=\frac{7}{3}
$$

$$
b=\frac{4}{3}
$$

The linear combination is:

$$
w=\frac{7}{3}u+\frac{4}{3}v
$$

Reconstructed vector:

$$
w=(5,1)
$$

The original vector is successfully reconstructed.

### Conclusion

The vector w can be expressed as a linear combination of u and v.

The coefficients a and b describe how much each vector contributes to the resulting vector.

This exercise demonstrates how linear combinations connect algebraic calculations with the geometric representation of vectors.
