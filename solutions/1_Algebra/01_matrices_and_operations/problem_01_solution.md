# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

## Given

The following matrices are given:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Questions

1. State the sizes of matrices \(A\) and \(B\).
2. Read off the entries \(a_{12}\), \(a_{23}\), \(b_{21}\), and \(b_{32}\).
3. Write the second row of matrix \(A\) and the first column of matrix \(B\) as vectors.

## Solution

### 1. Matrix Sizes

The size of a matrix is determined by its number of rows and columns.

- Matrix \(A\) has 2 rows and 3 columns.
- Matrix \(B\) has 3 rows and 2 columns.

Therefore:

$$
A: 2 \times 3
$$

$$
B: 3 \times 2
$$

### 2. Matrix Entries

The notation \(a_{ij}\) represents the entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\).

For matrix \(A\):

- \(a_{12} = -1\) (row 1, column 2).
- \(a_{23} = 5\) (row 2, column 3).

For matrix \(B\):

- \(b_{21} = -2\) (row 2, column 1).
- \(b_{32} = 1\) (row 3, column 2).

### 3. Rows and Columns as Vectors

The second row of matrix \(A\) is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The first column of matrix \(B\) is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Final Answers

- Matrix \(A\): \(2 \times 3\)
- Matrix \(B\): \(3 \times 2\)
- \(a_{12} = -1\)
- \(a_{23} = 5\)
- \(b_{21} = -2\)
- \(b_{32} = 1\)
- Second row of \(A\): \((0, 4, 5)\)
- First column of \(B\): \((1, -2, 4)^T\)
