
# EXERCISE 9 – A SYSTEM FROM A SUM CONDITION

## Given

Find numbers x and y satisfying:

$$
x+y=10
$$

$$
2x+5y=32
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

### Step 1 – Write the System

We are given two equations:

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=10\\
2x+5y=32
\end{cases}
$$

### Step 2 – Express x in Terms of y

From the first equation:

$$
x+y=10
$$

Subtract y from both sides:

$$
x=10-y
$$

### Step 3 – Substitute into the Second Equation

The second equation is:

$$
2x+5y=32
$$

Substitute x = 10-y:

$$
2(10-y)+5y=32
$$

Expand the brackets:

$$
20-2y+5y=32
$$

Combine like terms:

$$
20+3y=32
$$

Subtract 20 from both sides:

$$
3y=12
$$

Divide by 3:

$$
y=4
$$

### Step 4 – Calculate x

Substitute y = 4 into the first equation:

$$
x+4=10
$$

Therefore:

$$
x=6
$$

### Step 5 – Check the Result

Substitute x = 6 and y = 4 into both original equations.

**First equation:**

$$
6+4=10
$$

$$
10=10
$$

**Second equation:**

$$
2(6)+5(4)=32
$$

$$
12+20=32
$$

$$
32=32
$$

Both equations are satisfied.

### Step 6 – Geometric Explanation

Each linear equation represents a straight line in the coordinate plane.

Rewrite the first equation:

$$
x+y=10
$$

$$
y=10-x
$$

Rewrite the second equation:

$$
2x+5y=32
$$

$$
5y=32-2x
$$

$$
y=\frac{32}{5}-\frac{2}{5}x
$$

The slope of the first line is:

$$
m_1=-1
$$

The slope of the second line is:

$$
m_2=-\frac{2}{5}
$$

Since the slopes are different, the lines are not parallel.

Therefore, the two lines intersect at exactly one point.

Their intersection point is:

$$
(6,4)
$$

This explains geometrically why the system has exactly one solution.

### Final Answer

The solution is:

$$
x=6,\qquad y=4
$$

The system has exactly one solution because the two lines have different slopes and intersect at a single point.

### Conclusion

The algebraic calculation gives x = 6 and y = 4.

The geometric interpretation confirms that the solution is unique because two non-parallel straight lines intersect at exactly one point.
